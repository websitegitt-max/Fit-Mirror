CORE RESEARCH THESIS · SEPARATE EVIDENCE FROM ACTION

One bad workout is not reliable proof. Know when to wait, and when to adapt.

Most fitness apps either follow rigid static schedules or overreact to every noisy repetition. FitMirror AI introduces a two-layer, uncertainty-aware decision gate that distinguishes biological day-to-day fluctuation and camera noise from genuine training adaptation.

Launch Live Mirror Simulator Inspect WAIT ↔ ADAPT Gate
1. Measurement Uncertainty
Did the camera see clearly?

Tracks landmark occlusion, lighting, and joint-angle confidence before trusting a rep count.

2. State Uncertainty
Is the pattern repeatable?

Separates sleep, recovery, and daily variance from true shifts in individual exercise response.

3. Decision Uncertainty
Does benefit outweigh risk?

Applies stricter evidence thresholds to load/progression changes than to simple rest tweaks.

05 · Unified Action Space (Research Slide 06)

Personalized Workout Planner & Risk-Tiered Constraints

FitMirror AI does not use a single "make harder/easier" button. Each workout variable has its own evidence threshold ($\tau$) and safety constraint.

01 · REST DURATION τ = 0.65

Extend or Shorten Recovery

Lowest consequence action. Can adapt after 3–4 consistent sets showing fatigue drop.

Bounds: 45s – 180s
02 · SETS & REPS τ = 0.75

Adjust Training Volume

Requires repeatable completion & acceptable form across multiple sessions.

Max Step: ±2 reps / session
03 · INTENSITY / LOAD τ = 0.82

Modify Challenge Safely

High evidence requirement. Never increases if form confidence is low or RPE > 8.

Max Step: +2.5 kg or Tempo
04 · PROGRESSION τ = 0.85

Advance or Regress Variant

Single good session is weak evidence. Requires sustained mastery across ≥4 sessions.

Rule: User Confirmation Req.
05 · EXERCISE SWAP Safety / τ=0.80

Substitute Movement

Triggered by mobility limits, equipment constraints, or persistent discomfort.

Never Overrides Pain Flag
